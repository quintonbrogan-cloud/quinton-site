About

I’m a cinematographer drawn to natural light and the craft of creating it. My work pairs cinematic polish with a grounded, human sensibility. Every frame is considered, with lighting, composition, and movement serving the story.

I approach each project with a clear visual point of view and a collaborative spirit. From the first conversation through the final shot, I work closely with directors and crews to create images that feel distinctive, intentional, and effortless.

At home, I’m outnumbered by four kids under ten. Staying calm, adapting quickly, and keeping everyone moving in the same direction comes with the territory.

A set is still my favorite place to be. The early call, the first frame, and a crew bringing a shared vision to life. That’s where I do my best work.

Off set, I play sad boy music. On set, you’re fully allowed to make fun of my cowboy boots, which are usually unzipped.

Can’t wait to see you on set.
