About

I’m a DP with a thing for natural light, or at least the illusion of it. I like images that feel honest. Beautiful, but never distracting.

Whether it’s a run and gun doc or a carefully lit commercial, the goal is the same. Make it real, make it compelling, and make sure nobody notices the lights, even when they’re everywhere.

At home I’m outnumbered by four kids under ten. So when video village has notes, we’re losing light, and the grips are getting grumpy before lunch, that’s honestly the calm part of my day.

A set is my favorite place to be. The early call, the first shot of the day, a whole crew pulling in the same direction. I never get tired of it.

Off set, I play sad boy music. On set, you’re fully allowed to make fun of my cowboy boots, which are usually unzipped.

Can’t wait to see you on set.
